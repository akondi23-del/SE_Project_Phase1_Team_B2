Project Details

Problem Statement:

Bank branches in Albania rely on traditional physical queuing systems where customers arrive, take a token number, and wait an average of 30 to 90 minutes before being served. This causes significant wasted time for customers, overcrowded waiting areas, unpredictable service flow for staff, and overall low customer satisfaction. The root cause, identified through the DMAIC methodology, is the complete absence of a structured digital appointment system.

Proposed Solution:

QueueBank MS is a web-based Smart Bank Appointment Reservation System that eliminates physical queuing entirely. Customers visit the website, select a banking service, choose an available date and exact time slot, enter their details, and instantly receive a unique confirmation code. Upon arrival at the branch at their reserved time, they are served immediately with no waiting. The system also includes QueueBot, an AI-powered chatbot that answers customer questions about services, branch hours, and bookings around the clock.

Project Scope:

Aim:
To design and develop a fully functional browser-based appointment reservation system for bank branches that eliminates physical queuing, improves customer experience, and provides structured management tools for staff and administrators, built and validated using the DMAIC methodology.

Objectives:
- Identify and define the queuing problem through customer feedback and process mapping
- Measure average customer waiting times and peak congestion hours
- Analyze root causes of inefficiency in the traditional queue system
- Develop a digital reservation system that assigns exact time slots to customers
- Allow customers to book bank appointments online 24 hours a day
- Ensure customers are served immediately upon arrival at their reserved time
- Provide real-time AI assistance through QueueBot for customer queries
- Give bank staff a live queue management dashboard
- Give administrators full system control including staff, services, and reporting
- Monitor and control the improved process through dashboards and no-show tracking

Application Description:

QueueBank MS is a multi-portal web application accessible from any browser on desktop or mobile. It consists of four pages, each serving a different type of user, and requires no app installation.

Users:
- Customer — any bank client who wants to book an appointment online
- Staff Member — a bank employee who manages the daily appointment queue
- Administrator — the bank manager who controls the entire system

Key Features:

Customer Portal
- 3-step booking flow: choose a service, pick a date and time slot, enter personal details
- 6 available banking services with estimated durations
- Unique confirmation code generated per booking
- My Bookings page to view, search, and cancel reservations
- QueueBot AI chatbot for real-time assistance

Staff Dashboard
- Secure staff login
- KPI overview: total bookings, confirmed, completed, no-shows, and cancelled
- Full appointment table with search and filter options
- One-click actions to mark appointments as completed or no-show

Admin Panel
- Secure admin login with full system access
- Complete booking management across all statuses
- Staff member management — add and remove employees
- Service management — add and remove banking services
- System settings — branch info, opening hours, and feature toggles
- Data export for reporting

Home Page
- Public landing page with system overview
- Step-by-step explanation of how the booking process works
- Portal selection for customers, staff, and admin
